What's keptErrors (level error or status ≥ 500) and warnings (level warn or 4xx) are saved; info lines show in "All" while you watch but aren't saved. Errors and warnings are kept 7 days, builds 30.
GroupingSame project + path + first line of the message within 24 h become one line with a ×count, first and last time. Click a line to open the stack and request details.
LiveThe page holds one stream (server-sent events) that follows each linked project's current production deployment and reconnects every few minutes. The sidebar badge counts errors you haven't seen yet.
SecurityVERCEL_API_TOKEN stays on the server; the browser only talks to MasterMe. Only your own linked projects are streamed. Messages are trimmed to 4 KB.
