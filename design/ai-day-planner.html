What it looks atDue follow-ups, the next step of each active learning path, active projects, and (if you tick it) new radar items. Only titles, dates and status are sent.
How it decidesEach item gets a priority score and a "fits today?" answer with %. Deep work goes first, follow-ups get batched, learning after lunch, one block left free.
