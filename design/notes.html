What a note isA title, a plain-text body (up to 20,000 characters), up to 5 tags and a pin. The title is optional: an untitled note shows its first line in the list.
Order & groupingPinned notes first, then by last edit: This week, Earlier. Tag chips filter the list; All / Pinned toggles pinned only. The open note is kept in the URL (?id=).
AutosaveSaves 800 ms after you stop typing, and on blur or ⌘ S. The header shows Saving… then Saved. If a save fails, it says "Not saved · Retry" and keeps your text.
SecurityEvery note belongs to the signed-in user; all routes check the session and filter by userId. The body is rendered as text, never as HTML.
