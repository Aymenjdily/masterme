Principles

07
01
Amber means action

Amber is only for the primary action, the current state or focus. Use at most one amber button per view.

02
Sky is information

Links, new items and outbound sources use sky. It never marks a primary action.

03
Numbers in mono

Money, times, counts and stars use JetBrains Mono so columns line up.

04
Paper on chalk

White cards sit on a warm chalk page with 1px borders and soft shadows. Tinted text shades keep colour readable.
