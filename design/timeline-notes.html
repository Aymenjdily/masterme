When does a note leave the list?When it's already on that day, or once any block made from it is completed. Remove #todo from the note to drop it for good. Clearing the block brings it back.
Which notesOnly notes tagged #todo. Pinned ones first, then the most recently edited. The side card shows 3 and links to the rest in Notes.
Add by handAdd opens the free hours of the day you're looking at (the next free one first). The block takes the note's title and first line, medium priority, status planned.
Plan with AINotes is a new Include row. The AI scores each #todo note like the other items and only uses its title and first 160 characters. It never invents tasks.
PrivacyNote text goes to OpenAI only when Notes is ticked in Plan with AI. Every query is scoped to your account; the block keeps a link to its note.
